# Sample clips

These five EDFs are designed, not sampled. Each is generated by
`data/sample/build.py` (seed 0) and is under 1 MB. They are not TUH, CHB-MIT,
or Sleep-EDF recordings and they are not patient data.

TUH's data-use agreement forbids redistributing corpus excerpts. That is why
the demo is synthetic clips whose headers and labels match the failure modes
the library exists to catch.

| File | Why it is here |
| --- | --- |
| `clean.edf` | 30 s, 8-channel 10-20, 256 Hz, one `seizure` annotation. The clean convert. |
| `bad-units.edf` | Same amplitude (~tens of µV) but the header unit is `V`. `unit_scale` must reject. |
| `odd-channels.edf` | Adds CB1 and CB2. Drop and log; never rename to a 10-20 site. Also includes `sleepy-wiggle`. |
| `unmapped-annots.edf` | `tech-note` and `sleepy-wiggle` are outside the controlled set. Unmappable bucket. |
| `offset-annots.edf` | `seizure` onset is `10 + 1/256` s. Drives the off-by-one alignment test. |

`manifest.json` is what `eegh demo-plan` prints.
